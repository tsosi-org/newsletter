tsosi.org 1st anniversary

June 2026 marked an important milestone for TSOSI: we have been online for one year! TSOSI’s inaugural webinar was held on 16 June 2025 with notable speakers, including Joanna Ball, Niels Stern, Marin Dacos, Rosalie Lack and Demmy Verbeke. Participants discussed what TSOSI brings for infrastructures and how it can be used by the open science community. The first brick has been laid to support the different uses of TSOSI regarding the importance of sustaining open infrastructures. Take a look at the report written by Bianka Kramer and Maxence Larrieu on TSOSI's blog.

Shedding ligth on the funding network of open science infrastructure

In January 2026, we began to implement a multi-source approach: other than open infrastructures, we started getting data from research institutions and library consortia. As of today, we have added support from 7 research institutions, 2 open infrastructures, and 2 library consortia. It's a pleasure to put the supporters and open science policies in the foreground! We look forward to meeting TSOSI's future data providers!

Thanks to all of them, TSOSI now consolidates a 🌐funding network of open science infrastructures🌐 that links more than 1000 research institutions, 33 library consortia, 43 open infrastructures, and other entities.

➡️Explore this new funding network: tsosi.org/explore

Presenting TSOSI globally

During the 2025-2026 term, TSOSI participated in many international conferences, such as the openCost conference (Germany: DESY), the OpenScienceFair conference (Switzerland: CERN) and the 30th anniversity of the Centre Mersenne (France: Université Grenoble Alpes).

The goals of TSOSI, its context, and its blueprint have been written down in Katina and the ZBW Media Talk. It's always a pleasure to look in the rearview mirror and acknowledge how far we have come.

TSOSI keeps growing 🌱

We look forward to discussing with the international open science community. Keep up with our newsletter and LinkedIn page to be in the loop about what's planned for the coming year. Thanks everyone for your involvement and interest in making visible the funding of open science infrastructure!
