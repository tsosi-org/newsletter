🏛️ ROR hierarchies relationships

Precious parent-child relationship from the Research Organization Registry (ROR) has been added to TSOSI. In short, if support is given by an organization that is a “child” of another one, it will appear on both institutions' webpage at tsosi.org. Take a look at this brand new blogpost to see the results (spoiler: more than 80% of TSOSI's instititions have ROR identifiers 🚀).
