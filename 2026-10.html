Save the date: October 22nd webinar with openCost

This year’s Open Access Week theme is "The Cost of Knowledge". Together with our colleagues involved in the openCost project, we invite you to join the webinar "Focus on Open Science Infrastructures" on October 22nd, from 10:00 AM to 12:00 CEST. We will look back on TSOSI's achievements and challenges of the 2025–2026 period, and present the new data contribution channel, designed to help organizations share data more easily.

France’s new Open Science Strategy

The French Ministry of Higher Education, Research and Space (MESRE) has published its 2026–2033 strategy, and TSOSI is part of the strategy’s objective of internationalization, contributing to the worldwide open science ecosystem. This recognition highlights the importance of open infrastructures for building a more responsible and sustainable research landscape. We are grateful to MESRE, whose initial funding has made TSOSI possible. Read the Open Science Strategy announcement.
